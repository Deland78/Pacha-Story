## Narrative Spine Elements

These five patterns repeat and evolve across eras—track them in every Weaving:

### 1. Place as Time-Anchor (Tiwanaku / Akapana)

- **W01 (992):** Tiwanaku as living sacred system—burial roads, Kalasasaya, Akapana, ancestors embedded in stone
- **W02 (1992):** Burial road and dig corridor overlay eras during meteor shower; modern feet walk ancient procession paths
- **W03–W05 (1532):** Akapana where fever visions stack centuries (civil war boys, modern girls, deep past, far future)

> **Spine Function:** The stones are the archive; people are temporary readers.

### 2. Sky as Narrative Drumbeat (Omens That Open Pacha)

- **W01:** Comet three years earlier → first rupture and time-vision
- **W02:** Meteor shower → second rupture, modern echo
- **W04:** Eclipse → cyclical dread and intimate trauma transmission
- **W05:** "The stranger" comet → singular blade, conquest confirmed

> **Spine Function:** Celestial events are the knocks that open the door between moments.

### 3. Runners as Receivers/Relays of History

- **W01:** Khunu sees himself running toward a burning city—body in motion carries future catastrophe
- **W02:** Carlos sees chasqui-like runner in vision; Cusi plays runner in trenches (archetype as instinct)
- **W03–W05:** Nayra is a chasqui; even dying, his mind "runs" through time

> **Spine Function:** The empire trains people to carry messages; pacha makes them carry eras.

### 4. Fire → Seeds (Catastrophe + Continuity)

- **W01:** Fire as prophecy (burning city in Khunu's vision); ecological decline begins
- **W02:** Fire-visions of conquest flash into modern sky; living children sit beside them—seeds already present
- **W03–W05:** Fire becomes explicit (Cusco burning, "men in metal"); seeds become Nayra's last charge

> **Spine Function:** Destruction is inevitable, but continuity is chosen and carried forward.

### 5. Family as History in Miniature (Generational Trauma + Repair)

- **W01:** Communal burial establishes ancestors as ongoing participants
- **W02:** Household experiences the fold together; past enters family space
- **W03–W04:** Trauma chain: grandfather lost to empire → Nayra absent → son learns not to reach
- **W05:** Repair attempt: confession + urging son to "reach again" for grandson

> **Spine Function:** Empire's violence repeats inside the home until someone interrupts the repetition.

## Relational Voices

Voices are not "characters" but *threads of perception* that braid and unbraid through the story.

| Voice | Domain | Tone / Register | Mode of Speech |
|-------|--------|-----------------|----------------|
| Human (protagonist) | Kay Pacha | intimate, sensory | dialogue, thought |
| Ancestor / Memory | Ukhu Pacha | slow, echoing | dream, ritual fragment |
| Elemental Being (mountain, river, wind) | Hana or Kay | cyclic, impersonal | sound, silence, symbol |
| Collective (community, ritual chorus) | All | rhythmic, shared | chant, pattern |

## Ethical & Aesthetic Principles

### Do

- Honor silence, pause, and return
- Let the land speak through detail
- Allow multiple times to coexist
- Write through reciprocity: every act changes both sides
- Use sensory language (sound, color, texture) as emotion
- Weave relationships, not outcomes

### Don't

- Force linear causality
- Treat nature as backdrop
- Collapse events into chronology
- Center human will or mastery
- Over-explain or rationalize mystery
- Build toward a single climax or resolution

> **Core principle:** Every sentence should *breathe with exchange*.
> Every description should *acknowledge its source*.
