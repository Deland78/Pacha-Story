# 1532 Weavings Plan: The Chasqui's Threshold

> **Story Outline - Condensed Version**  
> Three weavings tracking a dying messenger witnessing Tawantinsuyu's collapse

---

## Overview

**Setting:** Tiwanaku ruins, March/April 1532  
**Central Character:** Nayra - Elderly chasqui (messenger), now ayllu leader  
**Timeline:** 40-year arc (1492-1532) woven through dying moments  
**Core Conflict:** Personal death synchronized with empire's collapse during *pachakuti*  
**Key:** Death occurs at the same sacred site as W01's Elder (540 years later), with comet present

---

## Three Weavings Structure

### W03: Strange Winds from Distant Waters
**~1,500 words**

**Present (March/April 1532):** Nayra lies dying among Tiwanaku ruins. Grandson arrives breathless with impossible news of bearded strangers and civil war.

**Memory (1527-1528):** First reports of Spanish in Tumbes - floating houses, thunder weapons. The trusted messenger network carries unbelievable truths.

**Transformation:** Confusion → Dread. The communication system binding the empire now carries its dissolution.

**Ends:** Deepening unease; more crisis approaches.

---

### W04: The Sapa Inca Falls, The Comet Warned
**~1,800 words**

**Present (1532):** Night rituals with coca and chicha. Consciousness drifts between timelines.

**Pacha Folding Mechanic:** In fever state, temporal boundaries completely dissolve. Past and future blur together until they become indistinguishable. Is the man with strange metal tools memory or prophecy? Is the comet overhead from 1531, 1532, 992, or 1992? *Pacha* doesn't follow linear time - all moments exist simultaneously at the threshold.

**Temporal Vision Layers (Past/Future Blurred):**
- His youth running to Cusco / A future runner fleeing Spanish soldiers
- Huayna Capac dying of pustules / His own body failing
- Halley's Comet 1531 / The comet tonight / A meteor shower centuries hence
- Tiwanaku ruins ancient / Tiwanaku ruins being excavated by strangers
- Civil war between brothers / Conquest by bearded outsiders
- The Elder's burial he never saw / His own burial he witnesses
- Languages he knows / Languages he's never heard but somehow understands

**Transformation:** Grief/Anger → Recognition → Temporal Dissolution. Empire is mortal, like Tiwanaku before it. Land endures while politics dissolve. Time itself becomes fluid, circular, all happening at once.

**Ends:** Acceptance of *pachakuti*; seeing through to deeper patterns across ALL time, not just past.

---

### W05: The Final Comet, The Final Breath
**~2,200 words**

**Present (March/April 1532):** Carried to see open sky above Tiwanaku ruins. Grandson brings final devastating news. The 1532 comet appears - same site, same sky as W01's comet 540 years earlier.

**THE CLIMAX:** Comet blazes overhead as Nayra dies - personal, imperial, and cosmic death synchronized. Three generations of messengers witness.

**Reflection:** 40-year journey from imperial glory to total collapse. Passing the messenger role to next generation, but what empire remains?

**Transformation:** Recognition → Transcendence. Death as passage to ancestor status. The message continues in new forms.

**Ends:** Not closure, but opening - vibration across *pacha*, becoming threshold like Khunu in W01.

---

## Key Connections

**To W01 (992 CE):** Elder's burial → Nayra's burial at SAME SITE; Comet over Tiwanaku (992) → Comet over Tiwanaku (1532); 540 years apart, same sacred ground; Empires fall, stones endure

**To W02 (1992 AD):** **RECIPROCAL TEMPORAL PERCEPTION** - Carlos perceives echoes of the past during meteor shower; Nayra perceives glimpses of the future during fever visions. They "see" each other across 460 years at the same sacred site. *Pacha* folding works both directions.

**Three Generations:** Grandfather (dying) → Son (middle) → Grandson (future) - lineage continues beyond empire
